Responsible gambling

Betting should be entertainment. Our tips are opinions, and even well-researched bets lose often. Only bet what you can afford to lose.

Keep it in check

  • Set a budget before you bet and stick to it.
  • Use the deposit limits your bookmaker offers.
  • Never chase losses.
  • Take breaks, and don't bet when you're upset, drinking or under pressure.

Free, confidential help

  • Gambling Help Online: 1800 858 858, 24/7, or chat at gamblinghelponline.org.au
  • BetStop, the National Self-Exclusion Register: block yourself from all licensed Australian online bookmakers at betstop.gov.au
You must be 18 or over to bet in Australia. Wager AU content isn't intended for anyone under 18.
