About Wager AU

Wager AU is an Australian tipping service for NFL and NBA player props, priced in decimal odds for local punters.

Every tip comes from the same process: a matchup model, real bookmaker lines, and research into usage, injuries, defence and game script. Nothing goes out on a hunch, and every result is published.

The analysis videos run on TikTok at @pk_p1cks.

Wager AU gives tips and analysis only. It doesn't take bets, hold money or act as a bookmaker. You place your own bets with a licensed Australian bookmaker.
