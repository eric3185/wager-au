How a tip gets made

The method

The same process runs on every game day, for both sports. Here's what happens before a tip goes out.

Matchup

Start from the defences that give up the most, or the least, to each position, confirmed on the stat the bet pays on.

Real line

Pull the bookmaker's actual main and alternate lines. No guessed prices.

Research

Minutes, shots and targets, injuries and news, coverage and pace, and how the same role has done against this defence.

Stake

The depth of the research sets the units. Every result goes in the ledger, win or lose.

The checks every bet passes

  • The matchup holds on the stat itself. A defence ranked soft overall can still shut down one position. The bet has to be backed on the exact stat it pays on.
  • The same role clears the line against this defence. We look at how players in the same role did against this opponent, not just the player's own recent games.
  • The defence type and game script fit. Coverage, pace, blitz rate, the spread and the total all have to support the side.
  • News is applied. Injuries, returns, minutes limits and coaching comments are checked for every player they touch.

How stakes are set

Stakes run from 0.75 to 2.5 units on singles, depending on how much of the research agrees. Multis are usually two legs on lower alternate lines, priced around 1.70 to 2.20. Multis are overs only.

No tip is a lock. A well-researched bet still loses often. The results page shows every one.
